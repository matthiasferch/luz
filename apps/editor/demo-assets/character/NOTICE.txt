Skeletal Animation showcase

and were taken from the existing Sala locomotion asset exports. They are third-party

Included character: Alpha_Surface and Alpha_Joints from locomotion.entity.json.
Included clips: Idle, Jump, Left Strafe, Left Strafe Walking, Left Turn,
Left Turn 90, Right Strafe, Right Strafe Walking, Right Turn, Right Turn 90,
Running, Walking.

Preparation preserves the exported meshes, skin weights, skeleton, material graphs
and animation keyframes. Asset references are relocated, Walking starts automatically,
and locomotion clips loop. Jump and turns play once. JSON is compacted and animation
filenames are regenerated from their canonical contents. No external textures,
Sala furnishings, BlendKit or Poliigon content are included.

To regenerate from the existing local Sala exports, run from the repository root:
node apps/editor/tooling/prepare-demo-character.cjs

The manifest records SHA-256 checksums for the prepared runtime assets. The demo
build loads only those files; it does not read local source libraries.
