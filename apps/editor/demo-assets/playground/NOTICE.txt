Rigid body playground

Original geometry and animation authored for luz, under the repository's MIT license.
This snapshot preserves the latest saved Blender export's scene composition, beveled
geometry, split normals, UVs, collision shapes and eight-second platform animation.

For public distribution, the local metal material is replaced by the bundled
ambientCG Metal 038 CC0 material. The environment uses the bundled Poly Haven
Studio Small 08 CC0 cubemaps with a black sky. See the corresponding texture and
environment LICENSE.txt files. No Poliigon or BlendKit content is included.
