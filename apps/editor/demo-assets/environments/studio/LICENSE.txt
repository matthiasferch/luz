source.hdr is the unmodified 1K HDRI. It is retained for reproducibility and is
not included in the public demo build.

radiance.ktx and irradiance.ktx are derived from that HDRI using the luz Blender
exporter's environment compiler, with 256 samples, 128-pixel radiance cube faces
and 32-pixel irradiance cube faces. Both preserve linear HDR values in RGBA16F.
Radiance contains eight roughness-prefiltered mip levels. No exposure adjustment
is baked into either map.

sky.ktx is an original 1-pixel black cubemap, also dedicated to CC0, which keeps
the visible backdrop black independently of the lighting and reflections.

Regenerate from the repository root with Blender 5.2:
blender --background --factory-startup --python integrations/blender/tools/prepare_demo_environment.py
Review and update the resulting SHA-256 digests in apps/editor/tooling/demo-textures.cjs.

These environment assets are CC0, separate from the MIT-licensed luz code.
